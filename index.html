    About Us

    Organization for Promotion of Girls‘ Sports in schools emphasizes gender equality in all aspects of life, including sports. Providing equal opportunities for girls in sports promotes fairness and helps break down gender stereotypes and supports education.

    Our Mission

    We strive for equal participation for girls and boys in school sports. Equal opportunity is the only way to discover potential. We envision girls as active participants and agents of change—equipped with confidence, skills, and knowledge to guard against GBV, unplanned pregnancies, and poverty.

    Our Vision

    A world where every girl in Africa has equal access to sports and education, free from gender-based violence and poverty—thriving communities where empowered girls inspire change and lead with confidence.

      Objectives

      • Mobilize young girls to participate in sports, education, and civic values.
      • Empower coaches and referees with sports skills.
      • Develop autonomy, discipline, teamwork, and leadership.
      • Provide economic opportunities and incentives through sports.
      • Reduce unplanned pregnancies and HIV/AIDS infections in schools.
