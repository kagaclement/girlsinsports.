About Us

Organization for Promotion of Girls ‘Sports in schools emphasize the importance of gender equality in all aspects of life, including sports. Highlight how providing equal opportunities for girls in sports promotes fairness and helps break down gender stereotypes and education..

Our Mission

We strive for equal participation both girls and boys in sport activities in schools because equal opportunity to be active is the only way to find the potential as women, girls and people as well. We predict a future where girls are not only active participants in sports but also agents of change in their communities, equipped with the confidence, skills, and knowledge to protect themselves against gender-based violence, unplanned pregnancies; Poverty etc

Our Vision

Our vision is a world where every girl in Africa, has equal access to sports and education, free from the threats of gender-based violence and poverty. We envision thriving communities where empowered girls inspire change, break barriers, and lead with confidence, ultimately transforming their lives and societies through the unifying power of sports.
